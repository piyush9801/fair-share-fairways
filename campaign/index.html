Three rules

Three rules, one result

1978

Proposition 13

Land a club has owned since before 1975 is still valued from its 1975 figure plus small annual increases. Member-owned clubs almost never sell, so they almost never reset.

1960

The golf-course rule

Nonprofit golf courses of ten acres or more are assessed on their use as golf courses, not on what the land would be worth for anything else.

1985

LACC v. Pope

The Court of Appeal held the clubs get the benefit of both rules at once. That remains the law.
